# Whiteboard Techno

Martin O'Leary (he/him) and Richard Sewell (he/him)

A whiteboard generates techno. Loud techno. Magnetic cards contain the building blocks of techno — drum loops, synths, air horns, a man yelling about hobbits — and a camera watches what you put on the board. Move the cards around to change the music. Is it a musical instrument? Is it a node-based programming front-end to the Strudel live-coding environment? Is it a piece of office furniture? I don't know but it definitely goes untz untz untz untz.
