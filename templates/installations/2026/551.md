# A Nixie Clock

This is presented by Peter Miller (He/Him).

A desk clock using Nixie tubes for the display. Nixie tubes provide easily readable digits illuminated with a friendly orange glow. 

Nixie tubes were commonly used for numerical displays on electronic equipment from the 1950's to early 1970's before LEDs were invented and became affordable. This clock uses a blend of old and new technologies - 1950's Nixie tubes, a modern microcontroller for timekeeping and operation. All housed in a laser cut arcrylic case. The Nixie tubes used in this clock are old Soviet-era devices bought from Ukraine. Further information on Nixie tubes is available from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixie_tube.
