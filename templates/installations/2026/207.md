# Cosmic Compass

Where is Mars? Like, right now? In relation to you, standing on this spinning ball of rock.

The Cosmic Compass helpfully points out where all the planets are (plus the odd satellite and galaxy) so that you know where to look when feeling awestruck by your favourite heavenly body. Jupiter is real, and it's *over there*.
Sometimes *over there* is down past you feet, giving a wonderful sense of cosmic vertigo.
Come press some buttons and know where things are.
