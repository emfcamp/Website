# Press Space Or Fire, Commander

David Mawdsley (he/him)

Lanterns of ships from the classic computer game, Elite.

Using the ship data from Mark Moxon's decomplilation of the BBC Micro source, the ships are made from 3d printed backets, connected with bamboo canes, covered with black fabric and lit with COB LED tape.  I was aiming for the wireframe (with hidden line removal) look of the original game.
