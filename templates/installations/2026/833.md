# Ready Freddy: The party machine

Freddy is a small 30cm cubed four wheel skid steer vehicle equipped with a floor facing laser projector and smoke machine for effects such as leaving a flaming trail, shooting missiles, trailing comets etc. Best seen at night he uses light up hoverboard motors for drive and a speaker for sound effects and music. He will be available for viewing during the day at the content creator village and will take tours of the site after dark.
