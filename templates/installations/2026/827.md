# Connection Established Zine Library

Connection Established was an exhibition which ran at The Photographers' Gallery in London from March to June 2026. It examined small web and alternative online practices within the framework of experimental publishing and zinemaking.

This digital zine library was created to run on a repurposed PirateBox as part of the exhibition. It's now landed at EMF! Connect to its wifi network to open, read and download any of the zines in the archive.
