# Thundergeddon - Multiplayer Robot Tank Battle

Thundergeddon is what happens when tabletop gaming, video games, and remote-controlled cars collide.

Step into a miniature battlefield where 3D-printed robot tanks fight for survival. Use your phone as the controller: navigate the arena, aim with live onboard video and unleash infrared fire on your opponents.

Bring a team of three, take command, and join the battle — come for the engineering; stay for the tiny tank carnage.
