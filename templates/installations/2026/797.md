# Stop The Clock Game

Nick Forbes
Pronouns: He/Him

Stop The Clock is one of the simplest games to understand and also one of the hardest to master. All you have to do is press the big red button once to start the timer and then press the button a second time to stop the clock EXACTLY 10.00 seconds later, what could be simpler!

Should you manage to stop on exactly 10.00 seconds the game display freezes on the winning time giving you plenty of time to leap up and down and make sure that all your friends or colleagues know how awesome you are!
