# Sonaforms

Sonaforms are beautiful, tactile wooden furniture that emit audio through vibration, allowing you to feel sound through your body. The forms and soundscapes are designed specifically to be calming and tell stories in new and unexpected ways. For EMF expect a few Sonaforms dotted about the site with different soundscapes. There'll even be a chance to feel you own sounds in some special sessions.
