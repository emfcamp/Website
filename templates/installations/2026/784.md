# Project Firewall

To protect us hackers from the evil outside world, we have come up with this post apocalyptic solution: a Physical wall of fire, hence Project firewall consisting of 12 to 16 self-built flame machines that shoot fire 8-10m up in the sky.

Lined up at a safe distance (5-7.5m) A spectacular site that some might remember from WHY. There it was located behind a water. Now we want to bring it closer to the audience, so you can feel the heat!

Installation has been used at WHY2025, Amsterdam Dance Event. This summer it will be used at 4 festivals including EMF
