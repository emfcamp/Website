# Befriend a Duck

Have you found a rubber duck on a stick somewhere around EMF? Tap it on the head with your NFC-enabled smartphone and befriend it!

You'll be directed to an online leaderboard that will be live throughout the event, and can compete with fellow EMF attendees to see who can befriend the most ducks!

No personal data is collected, and the only prize to be won is the joy of participation. This interactive installation is inspired and managed by your friends in the TildeVerse. Join us on tilde.chat!
