# The EMF Sky Survey 2026

Run by: Ben Cartwright (he/him)

A small collection of robotic and visual telescopes with the aim of creating a widefield mosaic over the period of the festival.
Also probably doing some solar imaging during the day and can present images and the telescope live capture as it happens
Also see for details for contributing: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rpwiPmgEqNA2OSQNGHYnVPFGSmSJcCDzyma6t7mbMc0/edit?usp=sharing
