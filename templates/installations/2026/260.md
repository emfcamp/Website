# Analogue Stories! (think AO3 but pen and paper)

Read stories and poems and see art contributed by other people!

Contribute stories, poems, and art for other people to see!

Make copies of the contributed stories, poems, and art to take away with you!

Make new stories, poems, and art! Based on, inspired by, or continuing the other art you find here!

So far this could be any online community after the mold of archiveofourown.org or built on Creative Commons licences. But everything shared here is shared under the brand new and terribly exciting Grumpystack Analogue Public Licence:

https://grumpystack.uk/apl

This means that you're free to take copies and build on top of everything here *so long as you don't use any kind of digital computer in the process*.

This means:
- No digital photos
- But you can totally use a pen and paper to make a copy to take away
- No posting on the internet
- But nothing you share here will ever be regurgitated by an AI[1][2]

Come read and contribute stuff that you want to share widely, and *also* want to keep off the internet.

[1] So long as no-one breaks the rules.
[2] Or at least, by a _digital_ AI. If someone builds an artificial organic brain, it will be allowed to read and share your stuff.
