# EMFEx - On-site point-to-point delivery app

Dom - he/him.

EMFEx (https://emfex.uk) is a delivery/courier app to use on site at EMF 2026, where people can request pickup and delivery of their items, and enrolled couriers can then accept the jobs and do the delivery work. Anyone can enroll as a courier.

It includes real-time tracking of the courier (if they enable it) as they're en-route, it uses the EMF map for easier navigation, allows for future/scheduled deliveries, notifications to the users/couriers/admins... and features a leaderboard of the highest scoring couriers (because why not gamify it!).

Crucially it only allows for pickup/delivery locations within the main EMF Camp boundary, and does not allow them in any of the 'backstage'/no-access zones.

Obviously it's just a bit of fun (although could actually be useful as well).
