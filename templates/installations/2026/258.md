# Space Base aka. Diggers are fun

Welcome to a highly (in)accurate heavy machinery simulator where you have to repower a lunar base using totally suitable diggers and trucks. Shifting lunar dust (sand) has damaged the base and covered the roads. It's up to you all to get power back in any way possible, be it by transporting power cells by road or bringing back the old crane to lift them over obstacles. 
(Its a sand pit with rc machinery etc. Lets be honest, its really just an excuse to play with diggers)
