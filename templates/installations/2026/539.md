# Nocturnation: Open-source crowd lighting

I've been playing around with some LED wristbands from the Coldplay tour that I saved from e-waste, bringing them to life and getting them to respond to music. I've extended the concept further, and now it includes a protocol and an app that lets Tildagons join in too!

We can now control fleets of Tildagons and other ESP-32 devices running the Nocturnation app to respond to the lighting desk via DMX at a stage or dynamically respond to a director ESP-32 device! Check out our website for more info!

http://www.nocturnation.net
