# Giant Clues by Sam Game

James "theorbtwo" Mastros (he/him) & Jess "castaway" Robinson (she/her)

Combining the two, a pile of Pico mcus, a central computer, and a framework, gives us a giant game board that attendees can play cluesbysam on.
