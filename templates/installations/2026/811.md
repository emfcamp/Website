# Snapper - a mechanical arcade machine

Snapper is a new arcade machine from lushprojects.com. It takes inspiration from a classic video game and builds it as a playable electromechanical model. Snapper will appeal to fans of Pacman but with a twist that it is played in 1 dimension. The 1 dimensional game mechanic is based on Paku Paku created by the amazingly productive Kenta Cho (aka ABA Games).

Snapper has simple but addictive game play which will appeal to causal and serious players. The characters are animated with cute details which give it a true feeling of 1980s arcade games.
