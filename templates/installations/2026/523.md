# Queer Power

Name: Queer Power  Pronouns: All of Us

I stick Pride Flag Plugs to places you wouldn’t expect* because Queer Power is Everywhere, 

You can see my Instagram here:
https://instagram.com/queerpowerart

I've appeared in the Museum of Transology at their month long, 10 year anniversary display in 2025 at the Lethaby Gallery

Some of these plugs from London Trans Pride 2024 can be found there and still up on certain locations along Oxford/ Regent street and Piccadilly! 

Found as far as Copenhagen and Berlin

This project started at EMF it's a true commitment to seeing how far a bit will run, and not really saying no to anything.
