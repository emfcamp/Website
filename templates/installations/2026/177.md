# Virtual Balloon Rides

Using a drone camera and helium party balloons, I'll be running live-streamed, scheduled aerial video trips across various parts of the site. I'll have a couple of drone First Person Video (FPV) googles  for people to experience the ride, and I'll also be running a live web stream.  This will enable those with mobile phones to view the trip from any part of the site. The flights will start and end from ground level so viewers will get the effect of takeoff and landing.
I'm very respectful of everyone's privacy, so the live stream will be only available locally on the EMF network and I will not be storing any images or video.
