# DASA xeno-biology pod

He/Him Andrew Kemp

Step inside a woodland space-settlement-inspired research outpost where our resident explorer, Bucky the Deer, is cataloguing and sharing some of Earth’s strangest lifeforms. Using a series of large physical buttons, visitors can explore short animated mission logs covering remarkable species including fire-adapted pine trees, carnivorous plants, ancient ferns, giant cacti and the hidden fungal networks beneath our very feet!
Discover extraordinary adaptations that might be strange and alien even to other far flung biospheres far off in the universe!
