# Portal to Bornhack

A portal to another world much like our own, Who will you meet on the other side of the portal?

The portal is the result of a dimensional tear which occurred after a personal transporter went through the luggage scanner. It took a few days but the rebels have successfully contained the breach within this unit so that it doesn't spread.
