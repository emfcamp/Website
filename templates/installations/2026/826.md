# A fleet of mega lofi pinhole camera

I've made 10 long exposure pinhole camera from old air rifle ammo tins (6cm wide, 1cm deep). They take one picture a day. 
I'd like to install them around the hr site and every night I'll gather the photos, scan them and share them. 
They're ephemeral, fragile, wonky, made on my kitchen table and are not guaranteed to produce anything.
