# Spring Simulator

A miniature, one-person-at-a-time immersive audio installation presented as part of NatureNet. I use fresh, locally-sourced surround-sound birdsong and batcalls in an umbrella-shaped portable format to bring some of the mental health benefits of being outdoors, even when you are in a tent.
