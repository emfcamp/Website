# Solar Satellite tracking Cyberdeck

A unique tri-folding cyberdeck, powered by a 24W solar panel connected to a home made azimuth and elevation antenna rotator. Users will be able to pick a satellite, including the ISS and the antenna will point at and track that object ( when in range). Then if available any radio telemetry or uhf voice signals can be tuned into and viewed or listened to. (Likely with a bit of guidance) 
Should no suitable signals be available at the time, the SDR can also be tuned to other signals such as FM radio stations or on site walkie talkies.
