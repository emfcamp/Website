# Inside the Iconic Teletype ASR 33 Printer

Discover the legendary electromechanical teleprinter from 1963. See and hear this fully restored and running marvel with the covers off !  Learn how it works and hear about the restoration challenges I had to overcome.
There's a chance to handle some of the old rusty mechanism then have a go at typing your own message and walk away with your personal paper tape banner.
