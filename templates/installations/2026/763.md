# jigging.satellite.sinkhole

Created as a collaboration between illustrator Tricia Wong and creative technologist Erica Frost, this work combines a hand-painted canvas with responsive projection mapping and “space”ial audio.

This interactive installation integrates recordings from NASA’s Sounds from Beyond collection, including recordings and sonifications from the Juno mission’s flyby of Ganymede and audio captured from Mars by the Perseverance Rover and InSight lander.
