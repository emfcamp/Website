# GCHQ.NET - Great Camp Hexpansion Quest

An interactive challenge for participants to find hexpansions hidden all over the site. The locations can be captured by plugging the hexpansion into your badge. They will often be hidden in villages and installations so explore all EMF has to offer in order to find them all.

The live leaderboard will provide the current scores and scores and provide the option for directly uploading your captures if you have wifi issues. Info and scores will also be available online at gchq.net.
