# Fingertip Symphony

Tactile exploration is important in our world and we are attempting to combine these two things, by hooking interesting materials up to speakers through sound worlds (and contact microphones).

Touching, scratching tapping and rubbing the panels will create sounds which can also be selected using the button box.
