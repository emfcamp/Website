# Meshcore Printer

A physical manifestation of the messages passing over Meshcore!

A thermal receipt printer attached to a meshcore device that is printing out all the messages sent to the #emfcamp public channel (and a few others like #jokes)

Send a message on #emfcamp, hear the printer chirp, and see your words in glorious thermal printed text! (ASCII only as apparently thermal printers have yet to reach the 21st century)

By Wilko @ Maidstone Hackspace
