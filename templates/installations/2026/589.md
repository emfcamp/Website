# The Sympathetic Synthesizer #PhotoSYNTH

This isn't a musical instrument that you play, this is a musical instrument that plays you! 
You and up to three friends gain musical superpowers just by smiling at your own reflection. 
Each facial expression has it's own sound so try and pose like your favourite emoji. Tilt, nod and roll your head to choose notes.

Each band member has a unique role with special skills, so be sure to experiment until you unlock them all!
