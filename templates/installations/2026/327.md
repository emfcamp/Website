# Crew Status: Nominal

Three astronaut figures stand in the field in their suits. Their large spherical helmets display imagery from their last mission, including orbital telemetry, deep-space photography, star charts, and more. Something about the crew is not quite right. The feeds occasionally falter: a shudder, a moment of interference, a spasm, imagery that should not be. Whether all three crew members came back entirely nominal is yet to be determined.

My son's request for a hat like the Las Vegas sphere for Hat Village grew out of control and our village ended up with it's own Eye of Sauron. This is the further uncontrolled growth of a hat.

The installation is interactive. All sensing is real-time and local only. No data from visitors is captured, stored, or transmitted.
