# The·Grid

The·Grid is back!  The·Grid is a field of LED trees displaying entrancing patterns and effects.  Previously shown at EMF 2014 and 2016, it has been rebuilt from the ground up and is now bigger and better than ever before!  Best experienced after sunset.
