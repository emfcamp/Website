# The Knitted Dome

by Case (he/him)

Knitted Doilies are not just for drawing room tables. Crawl inside and gaze at the sky through the fabrics. Definitely not geodesic, but a dome nonetheless!

The designer of all these circles is Herbert Niebling, but the choice of fibre makes a vast difference to how they appear. Come find Case to natter knitting and yarn selection.
