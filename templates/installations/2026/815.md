# FA/TH/UR-6000

FA/TH/UR-6000 is the central mainframe of the spaceship - the RRS Arcadia.
All system data flows through it, illuminating its tendrils and panels.
At it's terminal you can see the directory of the portals to other worlds; which goes where, if they are online and active, a history of sessions and statistics of all activity on the ship. 
Sponsored by Pimoroni.
