# Space Pong - Two player action game

Two-player, non-contact interactive game.  Hit a virtual projectile (LED trail) off the ground and try to get it into space.  If you didn't hit it hard enough it comes back down to you to try again - if you did reach the stars, then it comes back down on your opponent's side for them to hit.  Can be played competitively to score points against your opponent, or collaboratively to see how many consecutive hits you can achieve in a rally.   But get the timing of your swing wrong and you'll miss!

Small, low powered RADAR module detects motion (e.g. hands/arms/legs) to trigger the projectile - the faster the motion the the faster and higher it goes.  Space themed audio feedback is in sync with the visual effects.  Best played in the evening when the colourful LEDs have most impact.
