# Spark life - A collection of high voltage equipment and demonstrations

A collection and demonstrations of classic high voltage machines :-

Static machines like Van de Graff Generators and Wimshurst Machines

Electronic machines like small plasma generators and medium/small classic,electronic,valve Tesla coils 

Historic Victorian/Edwardian Electrotherapy machines. 

As well as some lesser known equipment like the Dirod machine and Shakesphere machines.

The aim is to show the above working, explain their operation, history and the science behind them.

Live demonstrations of the bigger machines would be most likely to run in the evenings when it's darker.

and an open invitation to other EMF guests to bring and demonstrate their equipment (after a safety check)
