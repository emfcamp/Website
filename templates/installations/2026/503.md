# RAMble City Farm

Created by: Jodi Crisp

Meet the animals at RAMble City Farm!

There's a sanctuary for retired computer mice, where the mice play happily and nibble on Cheese++, as well as a field of RAM grazing, a swarm of Bs that have flown in from old keyboards, and a bug hotel (with bug reports to examine.)
