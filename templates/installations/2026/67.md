# The Friend Finder 5000

LED banner screens, each advertising a single mastodon username at a time. Interactive in that any attendee standing next to one can take over that screen by messaging a mastodon bot, and their username will be advertised until someone else comes along. Intended to help attendees discover each other ❤️
