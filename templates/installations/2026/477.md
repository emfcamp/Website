# Tildagon MusicJam

1. Bring a Tildagon
2. Use the MusicJam Tildagon app
3. Choose an instrument
4. Jam with fellow Tildagon-havers!

The notes of each Tildagon are assigned to make pleasant-enough sounding sounds when played alongside any other Tildagon. The Tildagon app "MusicJam" (available in the Tildagon app store) will auto-connect you to the music jam!
