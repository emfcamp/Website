# Space Is The Place (For A Badge)

Over 150 small pin badges, taken from back issues of "The Fortean Times", all focused on UFOs/aliens/space/science fiction, with labels, being left around EMF for people to pick up, enjoy, and take home.

Because what's better than showing off your love of space than a tiny badge with the cover of "Chariots Of The Gods" on it?
