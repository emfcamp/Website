# Cliffhangers
## Rolling Barrel Games, 2026

**URL**: [https://rolling-barrel.itch.io/cliffhangers](https://rolling-barrel.itch.io/cliffhangers)

Cliffhangers is a 2-4 player party game about building an obstacle course across a giant chasm and reaching the other side. Players can choose between 4 distinct characters and rack up points by crossing the pit the fastest or killing other players with the traps that they place. At the end of 10 rounds, the player with the most point is crowned the victor!

### Platforms
Windows PC

### Technologies
Unreal Engine 5
