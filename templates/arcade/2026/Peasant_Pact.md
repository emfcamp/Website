# Peasant Pact
## Team 08, 2026

**URL**: [https://dumpling5462.itch.io/peasant-pact](https://dumpling5462.itch.io/peasant-pact)

Work together with your friends to defeat the waves of enemies in order to progress and save Avenor from the threats looming in the shadows.
In the realm of Bogvale, a great evil lich entraps the village of Avenor surrounding it in plague. Four unlikely heroes emerge from the gutters of civilization in an effort to save their aching village from the destruction of an evil lich, hellbent on destroying the land of Bogvale after an unfortunate inciting incident with four clumsy heroes.
