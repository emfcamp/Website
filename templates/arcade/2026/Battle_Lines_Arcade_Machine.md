# Battle Lines: Arcade Machine (BL:AM)
## Jim Purbrick and Jason Hotchkiss, 2023

**URL**: [https://jimpurbrick.com/2023/10/07/blam/](https://jimpurbrick.com/2023/10/07/blam/)

A 1 dimensional, 1 button, 4 player battle royale indie shooter.

### Platforms
Custom hardware

### Technologies
A Raspberry Pi Pico, a WS2812B 144 LED strip light display, some cable found on the street, arcade buttons, guitar pedals, an aluminium tube and C++.

### Child suitable?
Yes
