# MORSE
## ALJO Games, 2025

**URL**: [Steam Page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1976860/MORSE/ Trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bSLMGwQ2nsI](Steam Page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1976860/MORSE/ Trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bSLMGwQ2nsI)

MORSE is a minimalist strategy and typing game that teaches the secret language of Morse Code which you operate with a vintage telegraph wired up to a strip of LEDS. The Kaiser's fleet approaches under the cover of darkness! Use timing, tactics and telegraphy to illuminate and eliminate enemy ships. Similar to the board game battleships, you navigate the grid by entering letters and numbers in Morse Code then launch artillery to strike ship coordinates. After each enemy wave of attacks, you upgrade your arsenal, plant sea mines, new letters are learned and powerful enemy vessels emerge from the dark. MORSE itself has been developed as a tool to encourage folks to engage with amateur radio, so far I've toured the game with the Radio Society of Great Britain and plan to take it to the European Amateur Radio Expo in Friedrichshafen later this year.

### Platforms
Windows PC

### Technologies
Stencyl for the game engine, ESP32 and C++ for the alternative controller.
