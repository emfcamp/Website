# Dawnfolk
## Darenn Keller, Astra Logical, 2025

**URL**: [https://dawnfolk.com](https://dawnfolk.com)

Dawnfolk is a charmingly dark, casual and puzzly survival building game. Gather resources, rebuild your realm, and protect your people from oppressive shadows. Can you unveil the secrets of the monoliths and bring light back to the world?

### Platforms
Windows PC, MacOS, Linux PC

### Technologies
Godot
