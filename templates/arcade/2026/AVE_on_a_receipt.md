# AVE on a receipt
## Matthew Scroggs, Giancarlo Grasso & Katie Steckles, 2026

**URL**: [https://avegame.co.uk/play](https://avegame.co.uk/play)

A text-based adventure game on a receipt. Tear off and keep your game when you finish

### Platforms
Custom hardware

### Technologies
Python, a raspberry pi and a receipt printer
